Sabel-web2-Midterm.zip is Rick’s Roadhouse
Rick’s Roadhouse is a website made for a Roadhouse. This website includes a menu for the restaurant that is purely for display. Along with an about me section and a gift card page. The purpose of this website is to showcase my ability to create interactive websites, as it includes many interactive features, such as image resizing, a fading footer, text highlighting, clickable gift cards, and more.

Sabel-lp14-Final.zip is Morty’s Custom Cakes
This project is a website made for a company that creates custom cakes. This website's focus was on creating a well-made and working checkout process. The checkout process for buying cakes includes many customizable options like size, flavor, icing, filling, and message on the cake. After that, you select a pickup date and time. Lastly, you add your credit card or PayPal information for checkout. There is also a feedback form on the main page that’s optional. It allows you to suggest toppings, along with rating us on a scale of 1-10.

Sabel-Final-TTTT is Ted’s Tasty Taco Truck
This Website is a fully functioning business website. In which you can sign up, log in, and order food. You can add items to your cart, then sign up, then sign in, and the items will stay inside your cart. You can update personal information when checking out and in the settings. Also, in setting you can view past orders. Your past orders information stays in their even when you sign out or leave the page. I have an admin account where I can update item information. I can add and remove items from the menu. The Admin can also view all the past orders from everyone.

MidTerm-Sabel.zip is Dirk & Benji Realty
Dirk & Benji’s is a website used for selling homes and land. The Home page features a different property everytime you visit. Under Homes For Sale, you can view all the available homes. You can contact us or request more information under each property. After submitting a request, we show recommend propertys in your price range and type. For this project, I have an accessible admin page where you can add and edit homes. This project wasn’t made for commercial use, which is why the admin page isn’t safely proof. This page can be access from clicking on the bold text “Dirk’s & Benji Realty” at the bottom of each page. On this page, you can also hide and unhide homes that are available vs sold.
